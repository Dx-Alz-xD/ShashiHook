What this is

ShashiHook is a security research project that analyses email. It is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind.

It will be wrong sometimes

On held-out mail the false-positive rate is about 1%. That is a good number and it is not zero. A message marked benign may be hostile, and a message marked hostile may be ordinary. Do not use it as the only control on anything that matters. Published measurements — including the ones that are unflattering — are on the about page.

Your mailbox

Connect only a mailbox you are entitled to read. Use a provider app password, not your account password. You are responsible for holding the rights to the mail you scan.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use it to process mail you have no authority over.
  • The practice drills teach recognition of scams you already received. Do not use any output to deceive a real person.
  • Do not present its verdicts as certified, audited, or a compliance control.
